About

From the field to the dashboard

I'm a business analyst at McClelland Consulting Engineers in Fayetteville, Arkansas, where I've spent the last several years bridging civil engineering field operations and analytics. What started as fieldwork — geotechnical testing, construction inspection — grew into building the systems that turn that field data into structured, decision-ready reporting.

I'm currently pursuing a Master of Applied Business Analytics at the University of Arkansas, building on a B.S. in Business Administration (Information Systems). I'm drawn to the intersection of engineering operations and business intelligence: taking messy, real-world field data and turning it into dashboards and pipelines that project teams actually use.
